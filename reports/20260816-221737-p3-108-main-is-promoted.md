### 3 — Reasoning

**3A — ★★★ WHAT "PROD BYTE-IDENTICAL" MEANS FOR POP, AND HOW IT WAS TESTED.**

POP has no separate shipping binary with a pinned SHA — the reports have carried
*"Prod `<bin>` sha1: N/A"* since P1.2, because in POP **everything the build emits is the
deliverable.** So the invariant is what §2 states literally: *"build from a clean tree and
confirm the artifacts reproduce."*

Method: SHA-1 every artifact `build.bat` produces plus `probe.dmk` and `build/assets/*`; move
`build/` aside entirely; rebuild; SHA again; join on filename.

```
  artifacts in BOTH builds: 36
  with differing sha1:      0
```

★ **Two things that establishes, not one.** The artifacts reproduce **and** nothing in `build/`
was a hidden input — the clean build succeeded from an empty tree, which is the stronger of the
two claims and the one a same-tree rebuild could not have made.

**★★ AND THE 19 ORPHANS ARE THE FINDING.** Present before, absent after, so the build no longer
emits them:

```
  cel_image.raw  cels.raw  cel_pg4.raw  cel_pg4_disk.raw     <- superseded by the P3.78 split
  cut_page1.raw  cut_page2.raw  room_early*.raw  room_page*.raw
  proom.lz  proom.raw  proom_early.raw  proom_f{2670,2680,2686,3000,3600,4200}.raw
```

`cel_pg4` is the sharp one: the image was five rotating pages before P3.78 cut it to four, and a
`cel_pg4.raw` sat in `build/` for thirty dispatches. **Nothing would have caught it** — it is not
linked, not read, and not named by any check. ★ This is why §2 says a re-bake makes every
generated file stale at once and `BUILD COMPLETE` says nothing about it.
