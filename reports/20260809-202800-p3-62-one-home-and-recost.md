### 1 — Summary

**Part A: the duplication was worse than the record said.** `char_draw.s` held **two** bundle offsets, and the
second — `CHAR_TAB equ FLAME_BASE+54` — **had no use site anywhere and was also wrong**: `chars_tab` links at
`$302E`, i.e. `+46`. It had been stale by 8 bytes since P3.54, waiting for its first caller to jump into the
middle of a pointer. Being unused is what let it stay wrong; the live constant beside it was correct because it
was called every frame.

Rather than give the offsets one home, **the file was removed from the fact entirely**. `char_draw.o` is linked
*into* the bundle beside `blit_core.o`, so it now imports `blit_cel`/`blit_save`/`blit_erase` and lets the linker
resolve them. It was copying the *room's* mechanism, which the room needs only because it is a separate image.
One home remains, in `cutscene_room.s`, and it is irreducible — **and that one got the check that was missing.**

**Part B: the remaining beats do not fit, and the corrected scale does not rescue them.** Measured, on the port's
own timeline: **45 new cel variants, 26,386 B** of segment streams against **3,632 B free**. The whole scene's
characters would be **37,602 B against a 14,848 B window — 2.5×**.

**★ AND A MID-ANALYSIS ERROR I MADE AND CORRECTED, because it reverses the shifter verdict.** My first pass
measured only what a shifter saves by *deduplicating variants* — 7 of 55, ~4,102 B, 18% of the shortfall — and
concluded the shifter was near-moot. That was the wrong comparison: **P3.33 proposed raw bitmaps + a runtime
shifter**, and the dominant saving was never deduplication but the **representation** (2.34× measured). Checking
what P3.33 actually proposed, rather than my summary of it, reversed the answer.

### 3 — Reasoning

**3A — Made impossible, not checked, where that was available (AC1/AC2).** The dispatch asked for one home and
preferred impossibility over checking. Both were achievable, in different places:

- `char_draw.s` is *inside* the bundle → **impossible**: a wrong symbol fails the link.
- `cutscene_room.s` is a *separate image* LOADM'd to `$2000` that reads the bundle to `$3000` at run time, so it
  must reach in by arithmetic → **irreducible, therefore checked**.

`bundle_offsets_check.py` reads the `equ`s out of the room and `blit_tab`/`chars_tab` out of `build/obj/flames.map`
and fails the build on divergence. No third mechanism invented: `-DFLAME_BASE` still carries the base, and the
check reads the map the link already emits.

**3B — Seeded control, because a check that cannot fail is not one.** Restoring P3.54's `+58/+64` produces
`FAIL BLIT_TAB = FLAME_BASE+58 = $303A, but blit_tab LINKED at $3028 (off by -18)`, exit 1. That is the precise
fault that linked cleanly, booted, read the disk twice and stepped the VM before hanging — so a green link and a
good boot were never the check here.

**3C — A third home was in prose.** `flame_cels.s`, the file that *defines* the layout, described it as
"BLIT_TAB +58, CHARS_TAB +64" — both stale — and its header still gave `$0A00` addresses two relocations out of
date, calling the segment streams "the compiled cels" a dispatch after the compiled sprites were retired. **A
comment is a home for a fact that nothing can check and everyone believes.** The numbers are gone from both.

**3D — The recost, on the PORT's timeline.** Tracing PlayCut0's absolute timeline gives the wrong answer, because
the port drops the entrance and runs the final pair from `startV0`'s own x — so its positions, and therefore its
phases, differ. `beat_recost.py` traces the port's plan (`Vwalk 30 / Vstop 4 / Vraise 1 / Pback 13 / play 5 /
Vexit 17 / play 12 / Pslump 28`), stepping **both** characters per `play N` as the machine does.

| | distinct cels | variants |
|---|---|---|
| shipped today | 10 | 10 |
| whole scene | 48 | 55 |
| **new** | **45** | **45** |

Nearly every new cel needs exactly **one** phase — the corrected scale's invariance holds. The seven exceptions
are cels 48–54, and they recur because **`Vexit` ends `goto Vwalk2`**: he walks *out* on the same cels at a
different byte alignment.

**3E — The fit, measured both ways.**

| whole scene, characters complete | cels | + torch | + code | total | vs 14,848 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| segment streams (today) | 33,841 | 1,777 | 1,984 | **37,602** | **over by 22,754** |

Ratio measured at **2.34×** on the same cels. **This is a floor, not a total:** the hourglass (`flow`, GlassState)

intro's are.

**HARD-STOP #3 — no beat fits, in either representation. Reported, not designed around.**

**3F — Is the shifter needed at all? YES, and the corrected scale did not weaken the case.** It closes **19,202 of
the 22,754 B gap (84%)**. What the scale fix changed is only the *deduplication* half — variants per cel fell from
~2 to 1.15, so that half is now worth ~4,102 B — while the *representation* half, 2.34×, is untouched by the scale
and always was the larger term. **P3.33's conclusion stands.** But the shifter alone is **not sufficient**: still
3,552 B over before `shift_row.s`'s own object size.

Correction to the dispatch's premise, stated because a description in the record is not a fact about the code:
`shift_row.s` is **not assembled** — it is in no link script *and* `build.bat` never mentions it, nor
`shift_bench.s`. 12,953 B of source carrying no weight.

**3G — What the recost retires.**

- **P3.32's `Vstop` refusal (1,937 B over) — VOID**, already demonstrated at P3.58: 9 variants against the walk's
  own 12, shipped with 3,632 B free.
- **P3.33's variant basis — SUPERSEDED.** It compared candidates at 12 distinct cels / 24 items. Measured now:
  **48 distinct cels / 55 variants**, i.e. 4× the cel count and 1.15 (not 2) variants per cel.
- **P3.33's core conclusion — CONFIRMED**, not retired: raw + shifter is the only candidate that comes close.
- **P3.34–P3.41 (shifter cycle costs) — STAND.** They measured cycles per byte and per frame, which the scale fix
  does not change; cel widths shift by at most a byte with phase. P3.43's rebaseline and P3.55's cadence were
  measured on the running port and remain valid.
