### 3 — Reasoning

#### 3.1 — The reprise: own base, and why (AC1)

```
SilentTitle                        TitleScreen (beat 3)
 jsr unpacksplash    <- own base    lda #38 / jsr tpause
 jsr copy1to2                       lda #delTitle
 lda #20 / jsr tpause               jsr DeltaExpPop     <- inherits the splash
 lda #delTitle / jsr DeltaExpPop    ldx #140 / jsr PlaySongI
 lda #160 / jmp tpause              jmp CleanScreen
```

`SilentTitle` re-establishes the splash **because the prologue pictures overwrote
it**. Beat 3 did not, because at that point the splash was still there. Same visual,
different route — the same "who re-loads the background" distinction as
`PubCredit` vs `AuthorCredit` (P3.3). So the reprise is **beat 1's shape**:
`BEAT_TRACK` set, `BEAT_PATCH = BUNDLE_TITLE`. No new mechanism.

**Timing**, from the P3.8 oracle trace: `Prolog2` ends f7317; the title caption is up
at f7501 (**+184**); `SilentTitle` ends at f7811 (**+310**). `BEAT_PRE` is 178 —
184 less the ~6 frames the 5,909-byte title patch takes to draw, the same correction
P3.7 needed. `BEAT_HOLD` 310. No `PlaySongI`: it is the *silent* title.

#### 3.2 — CORRECTION 1: "re-composes from resident data" is the ORACLE's model

The dispatch's standing invariant says the reprise "does NOT re-read the disk"
because "the splash + all captions are ALREADY RESIDENT". **That is true of the
oracle and false of POP**, and the difference is a deliberate design decision:

- **The oracle** batch-loads its whole *compressed* stage into aux and expands from
  it (P3.4 §3.1). Its splash is resident.
- **POP inverted that on purpose** (P3.4 §3.2). The screen picture lives **on disk
  and nowhere else** — read straight into the framebuffer, **0 bytes resident** —
  because POP's uncompressed 26,880-byte screen is most of the oracle's entire
  compressed stage-1 payload. Only the *captions* are resident (the 9,216 B bundle
  at `$3000`).

By beat 6 the prologue pictures hold both framebuffers, so **the splash exists
nowhere in memory** and beat 6 re-reads tracks 27–33. The dispatch's own §1
prescribes exactly that (`BEAT_TRACK` = the splash) — the mechanism was right and
the sentence beside it contradicted it. **Two machines, two answers; the oracle's
cadence is not automatically the port's.**

#### 3.3 — CORRECTION 2: the invariant was already stale (AC3)

The dispatch asked me to confirm the both-buffers invariant holds entering beat 6.
**It does not, and had not since P3.8.**

`intro_seq.s` said: *"on entry to every beat and on exit from every beat, BOTH
buffers hold the clean base image."* P3.8 made base-only beats read their picture
**once** instead of twice — correct and deliberate, halving a 9 s stall — which
leaves the *previous* screen on the hidden buffer. After beat 5: front = `prolog2`,
back = `prolog1`. Neither is the splash.

Nothing had broken, because no beat had yet tried to inherit from a base-only beat.
The comment went on asserting the unqualified rule, **and this dispatch's plan was
written from it.** Corrected in source to what is true:

> both buffers are clean after every **caption** beat, and a beat may only inherit
> from a caption beat.

Beat 6 works precisely because it is a caption beat: it reads twice and restores the
invariant for whatever follows.

#### 3.4 — The composition proof (AC3)

The strong check, and the one the dispatch actually wanted:

```
PASS reprise == the SAME screen as beat 3's title: 30720 bytes byte-identical
PASS reprise removal is exact: 30720 bytes byte-identical
```

Beat 3 reached that framebuffer by inheriting a resident splash and stamping the
caption. Beat 6 reached it by re-reading the splash off disk and stamping the same
caption. **Byte-identical** — so the own-base re-establishment composes exactly, and
the repair afterwards is exact too.

#### 3.5 — The `Demo` boundary (AC5)

`jmp Demo` follows `SilentTitle`. `Demo` is attract **gameplay**: the animation
player and level tables — the same subsystem `PrincessScene` needs (P3.8 §3.2).
**`SilentTitle` is the last beat the caption/picture mechanism can express.** The
static intro is closed; the engine is the next arc.
