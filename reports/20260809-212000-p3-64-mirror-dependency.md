### 3 — Reasoning

**3A — Why this is a stop and not a scope reshape.** Four of the five beats *are* pure data and could be built
today: `Vraise` (67–76, 83–85), `Pback` (12–17), `Pslump` (1, 18), and `Palert`'s turn itself (cels 2–9) are all
drawn facing left, unmirrored. Building them would leave the scene wrong at **exactly the two moments §3 asks Jay
to gate**:

- she turns to the door and then **rests facing away from it** — the defect P3.61b already identified, now with a
  turn animation in front of it making it more conspicuous, not less;
- he turns to leave and then **cannot walk out**.

A gate on that would rediscover, by eye, what is already measured here. §3's gate is the *complete* scene.

**3B — Why the mirror cannot be data.** The oracle mirrors at draw time: `OPACITY` bit 7 routes `LAY` to `MLAY`
[HIRES.S:655-660]. The port's `blit_cel` consumes segment streams left-to-right; a runtime mirror would need
reverse traversal *and* bit-reversal within each byte, against a merge path already at a 6809 floor of 22 cy/byte.
So a mirrored cel has to be **baked**, which means:

1. mirroring in `sprite_convert.py` **before colour assignment** — the CoCo3 4-colour palette is chosen by screen
   column parity, so mirroring after conversion swaps orange and blue. This is new converter work whose failure
   mode is wrong colour, which is a **Jay-gated visual** (CLAUDE.md §3), not something a byte check settles.
2. facing-aware cel resolution: `walk_tab` is `[cel][phase]`; it becomes `[cel][facing][phase]`, and `co_variant`
   picks on `CH_FACE`. `vm_resolve`'s parity must come from `Fcheck ^ CH_FACE` at runtime — the fix P3.63 §8
   already listed, since `cel_table`'s parity column is generated for CharFace=left and is invalid for a turn.

That is **piece G**, and it is design work, not transcription.

**3C — HARD-STOP #5, invoked deliberately.** *"Do not start new design work at the end of a long session — reached
ten times, vindicated nine."* This session has run through P3.55–P3.63. A converter change whose defect mode is a
colour error only Jay can adjudicate is the archetype of what that rule protects against.

**3D — What the scene now costs, with `Palert` in front.** Tracing the port's plan with the opening restored
(`play 2 / play 5 / Palert / play 9 / play 5` before the approach): **131 steps, 56 distinct cels, 64 variants**,
against 55 variants without `Palert`. The seven mirrored variants are additional to that and unmeasured, because
the converter cannot yet emit them.
