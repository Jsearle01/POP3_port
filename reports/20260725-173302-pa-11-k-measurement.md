## Form B Report — PA.11 — measure `k`: translate POP's non-blit hot path to 6809 and count both sides
**Class:** recon / measurement — TRANSLATE + CYCLE-COUNT ONLY. wip. **Prod byte-identity: N/A** (throwaway translation).

> ## There is no single `k`. It is a property of the TRANSLATION, not the ISA pair — and the range straddles the decision.
> **`k_literal = 1.23`** — instruction-for-instruction transliteration. The 6809 is **23% slower**.
> **`k_idiomatic = 0.35`** — keep the sequence pointer in an index register. The 6809 is **~3× faster**.
> A **3.5× spread**, entirely translation strategy. The mechanism is concrete: POP's `GETSEQ` is a **37-cycle subroutine that exists only because the 6502 lacks auto-increment indexed addressing**. On 6809 it is `ldb ,x+` — **6 cycles**.
> ## The p90 frame — marginal since PA.9 — resolves both ways:
> **literal: p90 INFEASIBLE at 1.21×.  idiomatic: p90 FEASIBLE at 0.46×, 54% headroom.**
> **Feasibility is under the engineer's control, and this names the control.** Five dispatches carried `k` as an unknown scalar in 1.0–1.8; the truth is 0.35–1.23 and it is a choice.

### 1 — Summary

**The slice.** `ANIMCHAR` (`COLL.S:994`), POP's sequence-table interpreter — a dispatch loop of `cmp`/`bne` over negative opcode tokens, with handlers that call `getseq` (byte fetch + pointer advance) and `addcharx` (signed add with facing). Counted from the **assembled listing** (`obj/COLL.LST` `$4945`, `obj/CTRLSUBS.LST` `$D6F4`/`$D1BC`), not from source text.

**6502, counted:** `getseq` call = **37 cy** (jsr 6 + jump-table jmp 3 + 28-cycle body); `addcharx` = **31 cy**; the common **`chx`** path = **115 cy**; the **`goto`** path = **146 cy**.

**Two translations, deliberately.** A single number would have been consumed as a constant and would have been misleading, because the two defensible translations differ by 3.5×:

- **Literal** — mirror the 6502 structure, `CharSeq` stays in direct page. The 6809 must load the pointer into a register before dereferencing (it has no one-instruction twin for `lda (zp),y`), so `getseq` costs **47 cy vs 37**. `chx` = 137, `goto` = 185. **k = 1.23.**
- **Idiomatic** — the sequence pointer lives in `X` across the interpreter, so the entire `getseq` subroutine collapses to **`ldb ,x+` = 6 cycles**. `chx` = 49, `goto` = 43. **k = 0.35.**

**This is legitimate, not a thumb on the scale.** `GETSEQ` is not POP's algorithm; it is the 6502's *workaround* for lacking auto-increment indexing. A port that transliterates it imports the original hardware's constraint along with the logic. The 6809 has three index registers and auto-increment addressing — using them is what a competent port does, and the win is structural rather than clever.

**The feasibility answer.** PA.9 left the p90 frame marginal at 1.01× under an assumed k=1.0. Measured:

| case | k | blit | non-blit | total | vs budget | |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| typical | 1.23 | 17,099 | 129,860 | 146,959 | **0.82×** | FEASIBLE |
| typical | 0.35 | 17,099 | 37,103 | 54,202 | **0.30×** | FEASIBLE |

| p90 | 0.35 | 27,730 | 54,026 | 81,756 | **0.46×** | FEASIBLE |

### 3 — Reasoning

**Why counted from the listing, not the source.** §10 requires counting real instructions on both sides. Reading `COLL.S` gives mnemonics but not addressing modes or the jump-table indirection; the assembled listing gives the actual opcodes (`B1 49` = `lda (zp),y` = 5 cy; `20 03 D0` = `jsr` through the language-card jump table) and therefore the real cost. The 6502 `getseq` call is **37 cycles, of which 9 are call overhead** (`jsr` 6 + table `jmp` 3) — a detail invisible in the source.

**Why two translations rather than one.** The dispatch asked for "equivalent work". That phrase has two honest readings, and they disagree by 3.5×. Reporting only the idiomatic figure would overstate the port's margin by hiding an engineering precondition; reporting only the literal figure would understate it and might have condemned a feasible plan. Reporting both names the lever. This is also §2(c)/AC6's question answered directly: the strategy *is* the variable.

**Where the 6809 loses (literal).** Two real per-class deficits:
- **Pointer dereference.** 6502 `lda (CharSeq),y` does base-load + index + fetch in one 5-cycle instruction. The 6809 needs `ldu <CharSeq` (5) then `ldb ,u` (4) = 9. This is the single largest literal-mode loss and it is exactly what the idiomatic form eliminates.
- **Branches and RMW.** 6809 short branches are 3 cycles whether taken or not; the 6502's are 2 when not taken, so every not-taken `bne` in the dispatch chain costs +1. 6809 `inc <dp` is 6 vs the 6502's 5. `jmp` extended is 4 vs 3.

**Where the 6809 wins (both modes).** Also real, and they partly offset the above even literally:
- **`NEGB` = 2 cycles** replaces the 6502's `eor #$ff / clc / adc #1` = 6. A 4-cycle saving inside `addcharx`.
- **Two accumulators.** The 6502 must test `CharFace` with `BIT` specifically to avoid destroying the delta held in A. The 6809 simply loads `CharFace` into A and keeps the delta in B — the register pressure that forced the `BIT` idiom does not exist.
- **`rts` = 5** vs the 6502's 6.
Net: `addcharx` is **30 cy on 6809 vs 31 on 6502** even in literal mode. The literal-mode loss is entirely in `getseq`.

**Correctness of the translation — argued, not trace-checked (§7.2).** Each substitution is exact:
- `eor #$ff / clc / adc #1` → `negb`: both are two's-complement negation of the 8-bit value; only the value is consumed downstream.
- `bit CharFace / bpl` → `lda <CharFace / bpl`: the 6502 `BIT` sets N from bit 7 of memory; `LDA` sets N from bit 7 of the loaded byte. Identical for the branch, and A is free on 6809 because the delta is in B.
- `lda (CharSeq),y` with `y=0` + `inc CharSeq` (+carry into `CharSeq+1`) → `ldb ,x+`: fetch-then-advance-16-bit-pointer, with the carry propagation the 6502 must do manually handled by the 16-bit register.
- `goto`'s little-endian two-byte load through `pha`/`pla` → `ldd ,x++` then `tfr d,x`: the 6809 is big-endian, so the compiled sequence data would be emitted big-endian by the build; flagged as a **data-format consequence** of the port, not a code difference.
**No execution-level equivalence test was run.** This is the weakest link and is flagged in §7.1.

### 4 — Verification (AC-by-AC)

**AC1 — the 6502 slice quoted with its counts. — MET.** From `obj/COLL.LST` `$4945` and `obj/CTRLSUBS.LST`:
```
GETSEQ body ($D6F4)                     ADDCHARX body ($D1BC), facing-left path
  A0 00  ldy #0            2              24 43  bit CharFace      3
  B1 49  lda (CharSeq),y   5              10 05  bpl :right        2  (not taken)
  48     pha               3              49 FF  eor #$ff          2
  E6 49  inc CharSeq       5              18     clc               2
  D0 02  bne :done         3  (taken)     69 01  adc #1            2
  68     pla               4              18     clc               2
  60     rts               6              65 41  adc CharX         3
                          28                60     rts               6
  + jsr 6 + jmp-table 3 = 37 cy                                    22   + 6 + 3 = 31 cy

chx  path = 37 (getseq) + 2 (cmp) + 2 (bne not taken) + 37 (getseq) + 31 (addcharx)
          + 3 (sta CharX) + 3 (jmp) = 115 cy
goto path = 37 + 3x(cmp+bne taken =5) + (cmp+bne not taken =4) + 37 + 3 (pha)
          + 37 + 3 + 4 (pla) + 3 + 3 = 146 cy
```
MOS-6502 timings: imm 2, zp 3, zp-RMW 5, `(zp),y` 5, branch 2/3, `jsr` 6, `rts` 6, `pha` 3, `pla` 4, `jmp` abs 3.

**AC2 — the 6809 translation with per-class rules. — MET.** Choices and their justification:

| 6502 | 6809 | rule / reason |
|---|---|---|
| `lda (CharSeq),y`, y=0 | *literal:* `ldu <CharSeq` + `ldb ,u` | no single-instruction twin for indirect-indexed |
| `lda (…),y` + `inc`+carry | *idiomatic:* `ldb ,x+` | auto-increment indexed — the whole point |
| `eor #$ff / clc / adc #1` | `negb` | two's-complement negate, 6→2 cy |
| `bit CharFace / bpl` | `lda <CharFace / bpl` | B holds the delta, so A is free; `BITB` is AND, not bit7-of-memory — **not** the right twin |
| `clc / adc CharX` | `addb <CharX` | 6809 `ADD` has no carry-in; no `CLC` needed |
| `cmp #op / bne` | `cmpb #op / bne` | B-as-accumulator throughout |
| `pha` / `pla` | `pshs b` / `puls b` | — |
| `goto` 2-byte LE load | `ldd ,x++` + `tfr d,x` | 6809 is big-endian → seq data emitted big-endian by the build |

Correctness argued per-instruction (§3); **not** trace-verified (§7.1).

**AC4 — per-class breakdown. — MET** (§3, "where the 6809 loses / wins"). **POP's logic mix is dispatch-heavy and pointer-heavy**, which is precisely the mix where the literal/idiomatic gap is widest: the dispatch chain costs the 6809 +1 cy per not-taken branch either way, while the pointer work swings 37→6. So POP lands at the **unfavourable end if transliterated and the favourable end if ported idiomatically** — the mix amplifies the strategy choice rather than damping it.

**AC5 — feasibility re-check. — MET.** Table in §1. **Typical FEASIBLE at both k. p90 INFEASIBLE at k=1.23 (1.21×), FEASIBLE at k=0.35 (0.46×).**

**AC6 — strategy note. — MET.** The dominant lever is **keeping the sequence pointer in an index register** (37 → 6 cy per byte fetched), worth ~3.5× on this slice alone. Secondary levers, not costed here: **jump-table dispatch** instead of the `cmp`/`bne` chain — with 15 opcodes the chain averages ~7 compares (~35 cy) where a computed `jmp [n,x]` is ~11 cy, so it would further favour the 6809 on exactly the paths that are currently worst; and **inlining** `addcharx` to drop 12–13 cy of call overhead. Both are production-engine decisions, **not built here** (§6).

### 6 — Reactive deviations

**6.1 — There is no single `k`; I report two and refuse to average them. (The finding.)**
The dispatch asked for "`k`, measured". The honest answer is a pair, because the two defensible readings of "equivalent work" differ by 3.5× and **straddle the p90 decision**. Collapsing them to one number — even a weighted one — would destroy the only actionable content: that feasibility is a translation choice. Captured as a pool candidate.

**6.2 — The idiomatic translation changes program structure, not just encoding.** Keeping `CharSeq` in `X` eliminates a subroutine that exists in the 6502 source. That is a legitimate port decision and arguably the *only* sensible one, but it is **not** an instruction-for-instruction mapping, and a reader could reasonably call `k=0.35` optimistic. Stated plainly so the number is not quoted without its precondition. The literal figure is the conservative bound.

**6.3 — Correctness is argued, not executed** (§3, §7.1). §10 flags "incorrect-but-plausible translation" as the failure mode, and I did not run a trace-equivalence test. The substitutions are individually exact and defended above, but this is the one place where PA.9's method (simulate and diff) was available and not applied.

**6.4 — Equal-weight aggregate over two paths.** §5.3 permits this if opcode frequency is not cheaply available. I did not extract `SEQTABLE.S` opcode frequencies — my PA.4 parse of that file was already found unreliable (PA.6a §6.5), so a frequency weighting would have rested on a known-shaky parse. **`chx` is the common locomotion case and `goto` is the most-compared opcode**, so the pair brackets the dispatch-depth range; a frequency-weighted figure would land between them and would not change either verdict.

**6.5 — The `goto` path exposes a data-format consequence.** The 6502 loads a little-endian address byte-by-byte through the stack. The 6809 `ldd ,x++` is big-endian, so **POP's sequence tables must be emitted big-endian by the build** — a build-side change, not a code cost. Flagged because it is invisible in the cycle count but real for the port.

**6.6 — The literal `getseq` is the entire literal-mode deficit.** `addcharx` is *faster* on 6809 even literally (30 vs 31). Without the pointer-dereference asymmetry, literal `k` would be ≈0.97. Worth recording: the 1.23 is not a broad ISA disadvantage, it is one addressing-mode gap, concentrated in one routine.

**6.7 — No idiom filed; ninth deferral.** No emulator ran; the authorship-ruling queue is unchanged.

### 7 — Uncertainty flags

1. **Correctness argued, not trace-checked** (§6.3) — the largest methodological gap, and the one §10 named.
2. **One slice, two paths.** `ANIMCHAR` is representative of dispatch+pointer logic but PA.5 showed the non-blit remainder also contains `GRAFIX` draw management (15.8%) and `CTRLSUBS` (10.0%), which have different instruction mixes. `k` is measured on the interpreter, applied to all of it.
3. **`k=0.35` presumes an idiomatic port throughout.** Applying it to the whole non-blit remainder assumes every hot path gets the same treatment. Mixed-quality code would land between the bounds.
4. **Cycle counts assume no page-crossing penalties** on the 6502 side (`lda (zp),y` costs +1 when the index crosses a page). With `y=0` this never fires here, but the `goto` handler's pointer reload could cross.
5. **The blit terms are PA.9's**, which carry their own caveats (erase scaled not measured; 6.44 an upper bound).
6. **CoCo3 double-speed is assumed** for the 178,968 budget (PA.8 AC6); at normal speed everything halves.
