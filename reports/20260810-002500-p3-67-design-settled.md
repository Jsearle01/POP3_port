usable for all the reasons `gfx.s:387-393` already records. At 15,872 B the milestone's whole cel set is resident,
so the mechanism reduces to: **map two blocks once, load the cels, draw.**

**3B — What the arithmetic rests on.** `$C000` to `$FDFF` is 15,872 B, not the full 16 KB, because MC3 pins
`$FE00-$FEFF` and `$FF00-$FFFF` is I/O. That 512 B shortfall is exactly the thing that would have been assumed
away; it is subtracted here.

**3C — Where paging comes back.** The hourglass takes the set to 16,728 B, 856 B over. Options at that point —
none of them designed here — are a third block with a real page switch, the hourglass in a block of its own since
it appears at one beat and stays, or the encoding question the 513 B overrun already raises. **That is a finding
for the beat that hits it.**
