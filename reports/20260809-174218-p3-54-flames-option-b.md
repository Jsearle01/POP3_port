### 3 — Reasoning

#### 3A — What was built

Torch 0 draws from `torch0_cels` (phase 0, 13×2B, px 112 — **unmoved**); torch 1 from `torch1_cels` (phase 1,
13×3B, px 201 — **corrected from 200**). All 18 cels emitted through `cel_blit_prep.py`, **each reporting
`replay OK`** — the tool reconstructs the bitmap over a background before writing, so an unverifiable cel is never
emitted.

`torch_step` now resolves the cel pointer from the torch's own table, **reads rows and width from the cel's own
header** (one home for the dimensions — the P3.25 stride bug was a save and an erase disagreeing about extent), and
calls the shared primitives in the order erase → save → draw. **Draw is last because `blit_cel` clobbers
A,B,D,X,Y,U while the peel primitives preserve them.**

`PEEL_BYTES` is sized to the **widest** torch footprint (39, torch 1's three columns), not to one cel — an
undersized peel writes past its slot and restores garbage without failing loudly.

#### 3B — The space, and why it beat the prediction

| | |
|---|---|
| bundle raw | 13,996 B of 14,848 |
| free | **852 B** (predicted 716) |
| freed | **668 B** (predicted 532) |

The extra ~136 B: the **third dispatch table** (18 B — a segment stream needs no per-cel erase routine), the
`torch_call` trampoline, and the compiled total being a little above the 2,163 B I derived from symbol gaps in
P3.53. **The prediction was low and I am reporting the direction rather than adopting the pleasant number**:
668 B is measured from `flames.raw`, and the figure that matters downstream is the free space, not the delta.

#### 3C — The second `BLIT_TAB`, and what it survived

`flame_cels.s` declares the bundle's layout by declaration order. **Two files then hard-code offsets into it:**

```
src/engine/cutscene_room.s:122  BLIT_TAB equ FLAME_BASE_TAB+58   -> +40   (I fixed this)
src/engine/char_draw.s:112      BLIT_TAB equ FLAME_BASE+58       -> +40   (I did not)
```

**What makes this worth recording is how far it got.** It linked without a warning — the constants are `equ`s, not
external references, so there is nothing for the linker to resolve or disagree about. The room then **booted**, set
its mode, **read both tracks**, reached its loop, and the VM **stepped** (`vm_due` advanced to 52). Only then did
`vm_frameadv` call `jsr [BLIT_TAB]` through `$303A` — now inside `torch1_cels` — and stop.

**The diagnosis came from state, not from staring at the diff:** `probe_frames` frozen at 0 while `status=3` and
`magic=$4B00` said the room was up; then `vm_now=49 / vm_due=52` said the VM had stepped and the loop had *not*
come back. That put the hang after `vm_nextframe` and before `inc probe_frames`, which is the character draw — and
the character draw is the one caller of the constant I had not touched.

**Both homes now carry a comment naming the other.** That is a mitigation, not a fix: nothing compares them, and the
next layout change has the same trap.

#### 3D — Why the suite is red, and what that does and does not establish

```
FAIL flame pixels wrong: 21 of 32 bytes checked
  torch 1 row 101 col 50: got $C0 want $00      ... all 21 are torch 1
  torch 0: 0 failures
```

**`verify_room_flame_pixels.py` builds its expectation by laying `converted.s` over the room OPAQUELY at a byte
column.** That is a faithful model of a **phase-0** torch and it still passes torch 0 exactly. It is not a model of
torch 1 any more: at phase 1 the cel is three bytes wide and `cel_blit_prep` emits the partial edge bytes as
**`SEG_MERGE`** — read out of the emitted data, `$03,$01,$C0,$3F`: mask `$C0` keeps the destination's leading pixel,
`$3F` supplies the rest. **The background is meant to show through there. The checker expects it overwritten.**

**What this establishes:** torch 0 is untouched, byte for byte — the requirement Jay stated twice. The flames
animate (198 bytes changing between captures, inside the torch boxes). The emitted cels replay-verify.

**What it does not establish:** that torch 1's pixels are right. **The checker cannot tell a correct phase-1 render
from an incorrect one**, so its failure is not evidence of a defect and its passing would not have been evidence of
correctness. **That is why no gate is offered** — putting it in front of Jay with "the suite is red but I think it is
fine" would spend his eye on my guess.

#### 3E — Why I stopped before the checker and the cycle measurement

Making the check phase-aware means replaying the segment stream — the emitter's own algorithm. **A checker written
to match the emitter agrees with it by construction**, which is the failure mode P3.48b caught in itself and P3.49
spent a dispatch undoing. The right version consumes the *emitted stream* rather than re-deriving it, and that is a
focused piece of work, not a patch.

Against that: this session has run **P3.42 through P3.54**; the change already produced one bug that linked, booted
and ran before failing; and the remaining work is a correctness instrument. **HARD-STOP #6.** The build is committed
to `wip` — which §2E defines as the in-flight sandbox — with the state stated plainly rather than reverted, because
the conversion is real, reproducible from a clean checkout for the first time, and 668 B better off.
