### 3 — Reasoning

**3A — What "live" means here, and why the model is deliberately pessimistic.** A cel is counted resident from the
first step that draws it to the last. Anything narrower means loading it twice, so this is the lower bound for a
scheme with per-cel granularity and it charges the *whole* gap between two uses.

That matters because of the structural fact the dispatch flagged. **`Vexit` ends `goto Vwalk2`**, so the vizier
walks out on the walk cels — and the model duly shows it:

| | live steps | span | drawn | idle inside |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| viz cel 48 | 0..104 | 105 | 10 | **95** |
| viz cel 49–53 | 1..109 | 105 | 11 | 94 |
| pri cel 18 | 41..109 | 69 | 68 | 1 |
| pri cel 11 | 0..34 | 35 | 35 | 0 |
| viz cel 54 | 32..64 | 33 | 8 | 25 |
| viz cel 67 | 35..40 | 6 | 6 | 0 |

Six walk cels are resident across **the entire 110-step scene while being drawn on ten of those steps**. The
dispatch was right that this defeats naive page-in/page-out — and the peak already carries that cost. Everything
else is short-lived: 48 cels in all, and outside the walk six the longest span is the princess's slumped pose.

**The peak occurs at step 82 with 8 items live** (segments) — mid-`Vexit`, where the walk six are resident for the
exit while `Pback`'s cels have not yet expired.

**3B — The shifter (§3), stated plainly and not wired in.** `shift_row.s` needs four things:

1. Assembly with `-DOBJTARGET` — **build.bat does not mention it at all**, nor `shift_bench.s`. (P3.62 §3F
   already corrected the record from "assembled, in no link script".)
2. Adding to the flames link line so `char_draw.s` can import `shift_row`.
3. **The table fill, which does not exist in it.** `sr_shr_tab`/`sr_shl_tab` are `rmb 256` — *reserved*, not
   initialised — and the loop that populates them lives **only in `src/harness/shift_bench.s`**. Linking the
   shifter without moving that fill in gives a routine that indexes 512 B of uninitialised RAM. This is the
   load-bearing gap and it is invisible from the file's exports.
4. 512 B of tables plus the fill code inside the window, and a cel pipeline that stores one raw bitmap per cel
   instead of per-phase streams — a representation change, out of scope here.

**Does §1's answer change this? Yes.** The shifter is no longer needed to make the scene fit. It remains the way to
cut disk volume 2.7× and resident cels from 5,631 to 2,828 B. Whether that is worth items 1–4 is a decision, not a
constraint — which is a better position than P3.62 left it in.

**3C — The verdict on this mechanism has now moved three times, and the pattern is the point.** Each move came from
changing the *basis*, not from new data: deduplication-only (P3.62 first pass, near-moot) → the sum (P3.62
reported, closes 84%) → the peak (here, not required at all). **Nothing about the shifter changed; what changed was
what it was measured against.** The number that binds is the one that matches how the thing actually runs, and for
sequential content that is the working set.
